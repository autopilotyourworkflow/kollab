# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Convert Cake's influencer-marketing team — primarily **account managers** who build TikTok KOL shortlists for clients under time pressure and must **defend every pick to the client**. Secondary users: team leads / founders reviewing shortlist quality and vetting rigor. They move fast, judge on results over process, and have low tolerance for black-box tools or demo-ware.

## Product Purpose

Kollab is the **judgment layer** for influencer selection. It turns a client's brand (read from their website) into a **fraud-vetted, explainable, ROI-aware shortlist** of Thai TikTok KOLs — sitting on top of licensed KOL data, with public-data sampling for prototyping. Success = an account manager produces a shortlist they can **screen-share to a client unedited in ~15 minutes** instead of ~6–8 hours, with every ranking and every fraud flag backed by real, cited evidence.

## Brand Personality

Reliable, straight-talking, quietly rigorous. Carries Convert Cake's *"no sketchy practices / piece of cake"* approachability with BCG/Meta analytical credibility. **Trustworthy over flashy.** The interface should feel like a calm expert colleague, not a marketing demo.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS-cream AI dashboards (warm-neutral background, gradient hero-metric cards, tracked-uppercase eyebrows on every section).
- Follower-count-vanity influencer tools that rank on raw reach.
- Black-box "magic score" products where the number can't be defended to a client.
- Hackathon / PoC energy: half-built states, mid-content spinners, mystery-meat data with no provenance.

## Design Principles

1. **Defensible over impressive** — every score, rank, and flag is explainable and traces to a real, cited data point.
2. **Honest by design** — measured / inferred / estimated provenance and confidence are always visible; never present a guess as a fact.
3. **Native to Convert Cake** — looks and reads like their own internal product (brand, type, tone), not a third-party app.
4. **Trust is the interface** — fraud risk and data provenance are first-class UI, not buried in tooltips.
5. **Calm under density** — dense, decision-grade information with zero clutter; the tool disappears into the task.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA contrast (≥4.5:1 body, ≥3:1 large text / UI); full keyboard operability with visible focus. Every animation has a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative. Bilingual: **English UI chrome + Thai (Noto Sans Thai) for the "why this match" rationale** and KOL-facing content. Never rely on color alone to signal fraud/verified — always icon + label + text.
